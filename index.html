Escalate when needed

  1. 1

    Steady yourself

    Pick a small step, rest, eat, or step away from your phone for 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    Tell someone you trust

    A friend, sibling or parent. "This week has felt heavy" is enough to start.

  3. 3

    Your Pace Circle or school counsellor

    Peer leaders are trained to listen and to connect you with your school counsellor.

  4. 4

    National Mindline 1771 (24 hours)

    1771

    WhatsApp 6669 1771, or chat online at mindline.sg/fsmh.

    CHAT, for ages 16 to 30

    Free mental health check-ins and assessments by the Institute of Mental Health. Visit CHAT
